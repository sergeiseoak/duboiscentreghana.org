So, what exactly is Chicken Road?

Chicken Road takes the old “chicken crossing the road” idea and turns it into a casino game.

You start on one side of the road. Every safe move takes the chicken forward and increases the value of the round. After that, you have a choice: stop and collect what is currently shown, or take another step.

Keep moving successfully and the multiplier gets more interesting. Hit a losing step before cashing out and the round is over.

In simple terms:

Choose your stakePick a difficultyStart crossingStop or keep going

It is sometimes called a chicken road slot, although that description is not particularly accurate. There are no spinning reels. The game feels closer to an instant cash-out or crash-style title, with the decision happening one step at a time.

How a round actually plays

The chicken cross the road game mechanic does not take long to learn.

Once the game loads, you set the amount you want to use for the round and choose a difficulty. The chicken then starts at the beginning of the road.

Move forward successfully and the multiplier increases.

From there, you decide whether the new amount is enough or whether you want to risk another move.

For example, imagine starting with GHS 10. After a few successful steps, the value of the round has increased. You could stop there and cash out, or continue in the hope of reaching a higher multiplier.

There is no requirement to finish the entire road.

In fact, deciding when to stop is the main decision you make while playing.

What does RTP tell you?

RTP stands for Return to Player. It is a long-term mathematical figure used in casino games.

It is not a promise about an individual session.

If a game displays a particular RTP, that does not mean somebody who spends GHS 100 should expect a fixed amount back before closing the game.

A short session can look very different from a theoretical result calculated across a huge number of wagers.

If you want to check the RTP or other game details, use the information panel in the version you are actually playing. That is more useful than relying on an old screenshot shared in a group chat.
