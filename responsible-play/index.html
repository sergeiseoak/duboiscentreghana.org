Responsible Play

Practical principles for keeping real-money play controlled and within an entertainment budget.

Keep gambling separate from essential money

Chicken Road is a gambling game. If you choose real-money play, use only money you can afford to lose after essential expenses have been covered. Do not use rent, food, bills, debt payments or borrowed money as a gambling budget.

Set limits before you start

Decide your maximum spending and time limit before opening a real-money session. Smaller individual stakes can still add up quickly across repeated rounds, so track the total amount spent rather than focusing only on one bet or one multiplier.

Do not chase losses

A losing sequence does not mean a winning round is due. Increasing a stake to recover earlier losses can increase the amount at risk without changing the uncertainty of the next result. Stop when the limit you set has been reached.

Take breaks and watch your behaviour

Step away if play stops feeling like entertainment, if you are hiding spending, repeatedly breaking your own limits, playing while distressed, or trying to recover money you cannot afford to lose. Operator tools such as deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion can help create a firm break.

Demo is not a prediction tool

Demo play can help you learn controls without risking real money, but a successful practice session does not predict future real-money results. Virtual credits also have no cash value.

Protect your account

Do not share passwords, verification codes, payment details or Mobile Money PINs with people claiming to provide Chicken Road predictors, signals or guaranteed winning systems. Use only the support and security channels published by the platform where your account is held.

18+ and local rules

This website is intended for adults. Always follow the age restrictions and gambling rules that apply where you are located. If gambling is causing financial or personal harm, stop playing and seek appropriate support rather than trying to win the money back.
